The Beginning

It all started when my brother began a networking class. I would hear him go on and on about data privacy and how big companies are basically farming us for information. The idea began to grow on me that maybe I should take a stand against this. And albeit a small stand. I started with my raspberry pi.

Timeline

The past

I had been aware of the raspberry pis' existence for quite some time. However, I only recently purchased one for myself. The reason being, I want to make a fun homelab. I have a background in computer and data science and having a playground at home with no disasterous reprecussions is a very nice bonus. I wont have to worry about my services having 100% uptime or data breaches and I can test out new ideas for automation and learning.

The present

  • Currently, I have setup docker and pi-hole. I would like to learn more about vpn's and the risks associated with portforwarding before I move on to my next step, WireGuard.

  • Along with pihole, I have configured a recursive DNS Cache using unbound. It was a bit of a hassle to set up because I am still learning my way around docker. Regardless, it's up and running now, my hurdle involved installing unbound natively instead of containerizing it. Then I had to learn more about the docker network and configure unbound to allow the docker network through. If I had done a little bit more research, I would have known to containerize unbound so that I could bridge the two which would lead to easier configuration of automation. (This is changed later)

  • Deprecated Idea

    The Raspberry Pi NAS is coming along quite nicely. I have just obtained three 4TB Wester Digital Red Plus NAS HDDs. With them I can use a penta SATA pi hat and 3d print a case for optimal cooling and aesthetic. I'll also be able to run RAID 5 configuration to help performance and allow for data redundancy.
  • I have forgone the idea of building the pi into a personal NAS. Considering the fact that I will have up to 10 active users at any given time, I have decided to go with a Dell Poweredge T320. With this addition, I am going to be upgrading the rig that I'm getting (Facebook Marketplace Special). I will be putting a Intel Xeon E5-2470 V2 SR19S 2.4GHz 10 Cores 20 Threads Processor in there. Should be plenty capable for my plans. With that, I am also getting 48GB of DDR3 RAM.
  • I successfully set up tailscale (temporary VPN access until I configure WireGuard) and it has been great! I was able to set my pi's tailnet IP as my global DNS Server which allows any device on the tailnet to have the Pihole benefits (ad blocking). Along with that, I reconfigured unbound so that it is in a docker container. The reason being is now I have cronjob scheduling to update the Gravity list for the pi, and update the containers at 4am every day. Also, any device can access my Jellyfin server and stream video's remotely! This is only the beginning, now that I have remote access to my homelab I can replicate services that I currently pay for, host my own servers for webpages or games, any much more!

The future

  • Deprecated Plan

    To be able to contain my NAS, I will be 3d printing a case for it. There is a design I found online that allows for hot swappable drive bays, and good cooling. My biggest concern with this NAS is that the Pi runs quite hot. Heat and HDD's do not mix well and I would like their lifespan to be as long as possible.
  • I will be documenting the T320 build on here! I plan on upgrading my home network security and also turning my old house into a smart home. Along with that, I am hoping to achieve full service independence (at least from subscription services). I'll be sharing my triumphs and challenges during this journey and I hope some people follow along!
